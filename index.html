Frequently Answered Questions

Technical specifications, matched scoring logic, and workflow integrations.

How is the Creator Intelligence Score (CIS) calculated?

Our score engine weights engagement metrics (20%), audience trust parameters (25%), dialect and regional matching consistency (20%), readiness (15%), and completeness (20%) using custom calculations.

Can I hook this up to my own CRM or Slack channels?

Yes! Switch settings to Live Integration Mode to configure direct n8n webhook web URLs. Executing workflow chains automatically broadcasts notifications to Slack/Discord dashboards.

What language systems does the vernacular parser support?

The pipeline parses and semantically translates transcripts in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Bundeli, and Gujarati, mapping context back to target demographics.
